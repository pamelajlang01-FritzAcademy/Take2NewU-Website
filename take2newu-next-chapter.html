Take2NewU: A Fresh Take on What's Next

Take2NewU has changed a lot since its relaunch. Here is where we've been, where we're going, and why we're looking at total wellness differently.

Our story

Wellness has grown into something much bigger.

Take2NewU used to be a place where we worked on helping people become better versions of themselves through training, hypnotherapy and the occasional article or book.

Since the relaunch, we have changed so much.

We now have an online wellness store where we focus on vitamins and skincare products. The goal was to help people feel good from the inside out, but that just wasn't good enough. We wanted to do more and help people with total wellness.

And total wellness is far more than just your body and your skin.

It's making sure your mind can relax. It's feeling more confident knowing you've got things under control. It's taking care of the different parts of your life that affect how you feel every day.

That's why we started a new division — Take2NewU Labs.

We've been looking at total wellness:

🌿 Nutrition and everyday wellness

✨ Skincare and personal care

💪 Movement and healthy routines

🧠 Easier-to-understand wellness information

💰 Practical tools for the financial side of everyday life

Take2NewU Labs is where we're developing practical digital tools such as NaturoIQ, Fundage, Gold Motion and more.

Some are launching now, while some are nearly ready. Others are still being improved. And we'll be sharing much more about each of them as they roll out.

We're also going to start sharing more useful information here. Expect practical wellness education, product information, healthy-living ideas, new tools we're testing, and resources designed to make everyday life a little easier and a little healthier.

Take2NewU isn't about becoming somebody else.

It's about finding a fresh take on what's next.

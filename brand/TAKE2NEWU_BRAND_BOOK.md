# Take2NewU Brand Book

## Brand Core

**Brand philosophy:** Every Day Is a New Take.

**What we do:** Wellness. From the Inside Out.

**Customer promise:** We do not believe in perfect. We believe in better.

Take2NewU is a wellness company focused on vitamins, skincare, and practical routines that help people get back on track without judgment or unnecessary complexity.

## Brand Architecture

- **Take2NewU Wellness** — vitamins, supplements, and wellness packs
- **Estivi** — fresh, approachable skincare
- **Bellezza Duratura** — luxury skincare for mature skin; staged until Blanka products are imported
- **Chizel** — straightforward men’s skincare and grooming

RAE, Fritz Academy, Kissed by Gold, and future software brands remain separate.

## Visual Direction

### Take2NewU
- Warm cream and champagne backgrounds
- Refined gold accents
- Charcoal text
- White supplement bottles with white caps
- Soft lighting, marble, linen, glass, and subtle botanicals

### Estivi
- Soft blush, warm white, muted florals
- Fresh, light, uncomplicated

### Bellezza Duratura
- Ivory, champagne, antique gold
- Elegant, mature, luxurious

### Chizel
- Charcoal and matte black
- Brushed gold accents
- Clean, masculine presentation

## Typography

- Display headings: Playfair Display or a refined serif
- Body copy: Poppins or a clean sans serif
- Product names: serif, high contrast, easy to read
- Supporting details: uppercase sans serif with restrained letter spacing

## Logo Use

Use the Take2NewU clapper mark with the full wordmark whenever space allows. Keep generous clear space. Do not place the light logo on a light background without contrast. Do not distort, recolor randomly, or add unapproved effects.

## Voice

Warm, practical, reassuring, and direct.

Say:
- “Simple routines for real life.”
- “Thoughtfully selected.”
- “A fresh start can be simple.”

Avoid:
- Miracle claims
- Fear-based selling
- Overly clinical language
- Shame or perfection language

## Photography

### Supplements
White bottle, white cap, cream or champagne surface, soft natural shadow, realistic scale, label fully readable.

### Skincare
Show real supplier packaging whenever possible. Use light spa textures for Estivi and Bellezza; use darker stone or wood only for Chizel.

## Packaging System

Every Take2NewU supplement label uses:

1. Take2NewU logo
2. Small `WELLNESS` identifier
3. Product name
4. One clear descriptor
5. Capsule/serving count
6. Series accent band

Series:
- Daily Wellness — champagne gold
- Beauty — blush rose
- Focus — muted navy
- Recovery — sage

Final regulatory copy, Supplement Facts, directions, warnings, net quantity, and manufacturer/distributor information must come from the verified supplier label before print.

## Website and Shopify

The company website is the premium front end. Shopify is the secure store. Both use the same colors, typography, product imagery, collection names, and messaging.

Do not publish Bellezza Duratura links until Blanka products are imported and verified.

What Fundage Does

Fundage is a Take2NewU Labs financial-management and education platform that helps customers organize business and personal finances in one place. It organizes, tracks, explains, and simulates; it does not cancel services, move money, place investments, or file taxes for users.

Dashboard & Transactions

Organize income and expenses and understand the current financial picture.

Budgets & Goals

Create practical budgets and track personal or business financial goals.

Subscription Tracking

See recurring costs, annual impact, renewal information, and potential savings opportunities.

Invoices

Create and manage invoices as part of the business-finance workspace.

Financial Reports

Review financial performance, trends, profit and loss, and cash-flow information.

Tax & Document Organization

Keep tax-related information and supporting documents organized by year.

Business Switcher

Keep finances separated and move between business workspaces with the Multiple Businesses upgrade.

Investment Learning Tool

Learn investing concepts with AI-assisted educational explanations and simulated-money practice before risking real funds.

The Fundage Investment Learning Tool

This is a specific, high-value feature inside the larger Fundage platform. It gives beginners a place to explore investing concepts, ask for AI-assisted educational explanations, and practice decisions using simulated money.

Fundage does not place trades, invest real money, provide individualized investment advice, or promise investment results.

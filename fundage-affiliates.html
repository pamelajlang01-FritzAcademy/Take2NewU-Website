What Fundage Is

Fundage is a practical personal-finance tool designed to help everyday users get more organized around money without making finance feel intimidating. It combines straightforward guidance, planning tools, cancellation help, investing education, and a simulator that lets users learn with virtual money rather than risking real funds.

The positioning is simple: Fundage is not about promising riches. It is about helping people understand their money, make a plan, and feel more in control.

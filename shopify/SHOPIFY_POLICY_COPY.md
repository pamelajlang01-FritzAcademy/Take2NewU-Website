## Returns and refunds draft

Because wellness and personal-care products may involve hygiene and safety considerations, return eligibility depends on product condition, supplier rules, and the reason for the request.

Contact take2newu@take2newu.com promptly after delivery before returning any item. Include the order number and a description of the issue. Do not send a return without receiving instructions.

Opened, used, or unsealed supplements and personal-care products may not be eligible for return unless they arrived damaged, defective, or incorrect. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method after review.

Shipping charges may be nonrefundable unless the order was damaged, defective, or incorrectly fulfilled. Final-sale items, gift cards, and certain promotional products may not be returnable.

The final published return window must match the actual supplier and fulfillment policies.

## Damaged or incorrect orders

Inspect the order after delivery. If an item arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, contact take2newu@take2newu.com promptly with:

- Shopify order number
- Description of the issue
- Clear photographs of the product, packaging, and shipping label

Keep the product and packaging until instructions are provided.

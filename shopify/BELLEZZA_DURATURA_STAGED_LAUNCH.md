## Product Page Template

### Product Title
Use the exact product name supplied by Blanka unless a compliant, clearer customer-facing title is approved.

### Opening Paragraph
A concise explanation of what the product is, its role in the routine, and the skin experience it is designed to support.

### Why You'll Love It
Use only claims supported by the supplier's product data.

Suggested structure:
- Supports comfortable, hydrated-looking skin
- Fits easily into a consistent daily routine
- Elegant texture and application experience
- Selected for the Bellezza Duratura collection

### Ideal For
- mature skin
- dry or moisture-depleted skin, when supported by product data
- customers seeking a refined routine
- customers who prefer simple, consistent care

### How to Use
Use Blanka's exact directions. Do not rewrite dosage, frequency, warnings, or application instructions in a way that changes meaning.

### Ingredients
Use the complete supplier ingredient list exactly as provided.

### Pairs Well With
Recommend products by routine step:
- cleanser
- hydrating serum
- moisturizer
- eye or targeted treatment
- night care

### Safety and Claims Rule
Do not claim that a cosmetic product:
- treats disease
- reverses aging
- rebuilds collagen unless the exact supported cosmetic claim is legally supplied
- removes wrinkles permanently
- produces guaranteed results

Prefer cosmetic phrasing such as:
- helps skin appear smoother
- supports a hydrated appearance
- leaves skin feeling soft
- promotes a more radiant-looking complexion

## Blanka Connection and Import Checklist

1. Connect the correct Blanka account to the Take2NewU Shopify store.
2. Confirm billing, fulfillment region, shipping expectations, and branding options.
3. Review the available product catalog before importing.
4. Select a focused launch assortment rather than importing everything.
5. Confirm actual packaging, bottle colors, cap colors, label dimensions, and mockup options.
6. Confirm wholesale cost, retail price, shipping cost, and margin for each product.
7. Confirm ingredient lists, directions, warnings, net contents, and required legal text.
8. Import selected products as Draft.
9. Assign vendor: Bellezza Duratura or the final agreed vendor naming convention.
10. Assign product type and Shopify category.
11. Rewrite titles only when needed for clarity and compliance.
12. Replace supplier-default descriptions with approved Bellezza copy.
13. Review every image at full size and on mobile.
14. Create the Bellezza Duratura collection.
15. Add products in intentional routine order.
16. Set SEO titles and meta descriptions.
17. Test fulfillment, shipping, taxes, and checkout with one product before activating the entire line.
18. Activate products only after the collection page and navigation are ready.
19. Add Bellezza to Shopify and the custom website at the same time.

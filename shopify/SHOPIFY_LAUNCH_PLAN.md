# Take2NewU Shopify Launch Plan

This file is the implementation source for the Take2NewU Shopify store. It is designed so the 30–45 minute Mac work block can focus on applying changes instead of deciding what to build.

## Product page standard

Every product page should include:

1. Clear product title
2. One-sentence benefit summary
3. What it supports
4. Key ingredients or product features
5. How to use
6. Who it may suit
7. Important cautions from the supplier label
8. Shipping and returns link
9. Wellness disclaimer

Avoid miracle claims, disease-treatment claims, guaranteed outcomes, or language that suggests professional medical advice.

## Product naming standard

Use customer-friendly names first and technical details second.

Examples:

- Calm Magnesium — Daily Relaxation Support
- Daily D+K Balance — Vitamin D3 + K2 Support
- Gut Harmony Probiotics — Daily Digestive Support
- Golden Joint & Recovery — Mobility and Recovery Support
- Mind Focus Pack — Daily Focus Support

Do not rename products until the supplier label, formula, and legal product name are confirmed.

## Mac implementation order

Complete in this order during the short Mac block:

1. Confirm store access and collaborator permissions
2. Open Online Store > Navigation
3. Apply the main navigation structure above
4. Open Products > Collections
5. Create or correct the five collections
6. Assign products to the correct collections
7. Open Online Store > Themes > Customize
8. Reorder homepage sections using the approved order
9. Replace homepage copy with the copy above
10. Confirm every CTA opens the correct collection or product page
11. Test checkout with no payment submitted
12. Test the mobile menu and one product page on iPhone

## Do not do yet

- Do not redesign all labels inside Shopify
- Do not add RAE or Fritz Academy
- Do not add unfinished apps
- Do not add dozens of new products
- Do not publish medical or disease claims
- Do not replace WordPress with Shopify

WordPress remains the main Take2NewU company site. Shopify remains the secure product catalog and checkout.
